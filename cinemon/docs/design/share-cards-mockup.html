35mm share cards, round 2

Each card is a full Instagram story at 9:16, drawn to scale. The app would export them at 1080 × 1920. Posters and stills are real TMDB artwork, and the review text is sample copy. Every card has a code so you can say "keep R1, push H2, kill T2". Cards marked New are the ones redone after your notes.

Full story fills the whole screen.Sticker is a card on a poster-colour background that you can move and resize inside Instagram, like Spotify's song share.

The share sheet

In the app

Tap Share on any post. Swipe sideways through that post's styles, choose a background colour taken from the poster, then send it straight to your Instagram or Facebook story, save it, or use the normal share menu.

Hot takes

H1 – H3

The goal is to start an argument. The vote split is what makes someone tap through, so each design shows it, and the text asks the viewer to vote on 35mm.

Reviews

R2 – R3

This is the post people will share most often, so it gets the most styles. Each one keeps the rating and a single line of the review large enough to read without zooming.

Episode reviews

E1 – E2

Episodes have a wide still rather than a poster, so their layouts are built around that shape.

Critiques

C1 – C2

Long-form pieces should look like something worth reading. They use a serif, like print criticism, so they stand apart from the quick posts.

Top 3 and favourites

T1 – T2

Top 3 lists are how people show their identity, and they're what friends reply to. T1 now uses the podium from your profile, with the same art and badges.

Profile cards

P1 – P2

These say "follow me on 35mm". Both are rebuilt: P1 is your actual profile header, and P2 replaces the receipt with a cleaner monthly recap.

Playlists

L1

Modelled on Spotify's playlist share: the cover, the title and description, then the first films on the list.

Film images and data come from TMDB, and the exported cards will carry a small "TMDB" credit. Sample reviews are placeholder copy. Fonts in these mockups: San Francisco and Helvetica Neue (both on every iPhone), the app's own Siberian and 3D Isometric, plus Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Mono, Big Shoulders Display and Reenie Beanie for the themed styles. Any of these would be bundled into the app.
